Choose where this browser keeps your records

Records are never sent to this site or a server. They stay on this device unless you export them. Only the date, matter, activity and summary scores are kept. Persistent storage lasts until you clear it; avoid it on shared devices. Session storage lasts for this tab. Memory lasts only on this page.

Practice milestones

Each milestone recognizes effort and progress in your own practice. Three matters or three feedback skill areas mark breadth. A later saved pass for the same matter and activity marks revision; a higher score on the same scale marks improvement. Saving the same feedback twice does not represent a new pass.
