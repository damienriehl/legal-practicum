## Purpose {#b:076e7234}

Use this questionnaire at the end of the course to reflect on how the practicum went and whether your objectives for practicing law changed. It complements the Reflective Report with short, specific answers you can discuss with your instructor and use to plan your next learning experience. {#b:86b7f7de}

## The template {#b:56dcb6ec}

Record your name and the date. Answer in your own words, drawing on examples from your matters, feedback, and work with your partner. {#b:aad9acf8}

### 1. How the course went {#b:e3ab4939}

What helped you learn most? Which parts were difficult to manage or understand? What would you keep, and what would you change for the next group of students? {#b:560f7600}

### 2. Progress toward your objectives {#b:2859d1f8}

What were your objectives at the start of the course? What can you now demonstrate that you could not do then? Give one or two examples of work that shows your progress. {#b:8ed44646}

### 3. Your objectives for practicing law {#b:2433026c}

Have your objectives for practicing law changed? Consider the work you want to do, the clients you hope to serve, and the responsibilities you want to take on. What experience influenced any change? If your objectives stayed the same, what reinforced them? {#b:6c435d6c}

### 4. Practice, feedback, and partnership {#b:5b7afd42}

How did your weekly preparation and revisions affect your learning? Which feedback helped you move forward? What did working with your partner teach you about sharing responsibility? {#b:bfbd20ea}

### 5. Your next steps {#b:18fce294}

Which skill or habit needs more practice? Name a specific next step, when you will take it, and how you will know you are making progress. What support would help? {#b:b03a9436}

## Assessment and feedback {#b:cdb4247e}

Demonstrate reflection through concrete examples and honest connections between your experience and your objectives. Use the discussion to recognize what to keep doing and identify practical next steps for your development. {#b:c97daf16}
