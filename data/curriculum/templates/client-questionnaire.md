## Purpose {#b:b2d1727f}

Prepare a questionnaire for each client alongside your Client-Interview Plan. Adapt it to the matter and the person you will meet. It helps you identify what you need to ask and what documents to request; the interview gives the client room to explain the answers in their own words. {#b:dea3d907}

## The template {#b:65526027}

Record the matter, the client's name and role, your name, and the date. Invite the client to leave uncertain answers open and discuss sensitive questions with you during the interview. {#b:30d9409e}

### 1. Contact and communication {#b:dd5fc8ff}

How would you like us to contact you? Are there times or ways of communicating that we should avoid? What would help you take part comfortably in our meetings? {#b:70a8f98f}

### 2. What brought you here {#b:60f719c2}

Please describe what happened in your own words. When did the important events occur? Who was involved, and who else may know something about them? {#b:a44dd899}

### 3. Your objectives and concerns {#b:909cbfcd}

What would you like to happen next? What matters most to you? How has the situation affected your work, finances, relationships, or daily life? Is there anything you are especially worried about? {#b:b0efb9b4}

### 4. Documents and deadlines {#b:909ca2ad}

What letters, messages, agreements, photographs, or other records do you have? Have you received a notice or been asked to respond by a particular date? Please identify the records and dates so we can review them together. {#b:c7d9f301}

### 5. Earlier steps and other people {#b:dad07897}

What have you already tried? Have you spoken with another lawyer or anyone else about this matter? Is another party represented by a lawyer? Include names for the firm's conflicts check. {#b:a53888a0}

### 6. Questions for us {#b:d059d738}

What would you like to ask about the process, fees, or our work together? What else should we understand before planning the next step? {#b:c3b08a17}

## Assessment and feedback {#b:e37b0f0b}

Demonstrate preparation by tailoring the questionnaire to the client and matter, using plain questions, and leaving room for uncertainty. Use the answers to refine your interview objectives and topic map. Confirm important details in conversation rather than assuming a written answer is complete. {#b:70e2596d}
